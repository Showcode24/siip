1

Register and build your profile

Sign up free, tell us about your degree, NYSC status and skills, and upload your CV. Your profile strength shows what to add next.

2

Take the screening test

A short, timed test, scored instantly and fairly. Then our team reviews your application.

3

Train for real work

Practical courses in digital, office and workplace skills. Learn at your own pace on your phone, and pick up where you stopped.

4

Pass the final assessment

Show what you've learned. Pass, and you qualify for placement with a partner company.

5

Get matched to a company

We match you to openings by skills, discipline, location and availability, then the company accepts you.

6

Do a three-month internship

Work with a supervisor, share monthly check-ins, and some interns are offered permanent roles.

7

Earn a verified certificate

Employers can confirm it's genuine in seconds with your verification code.
