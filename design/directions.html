Three directions built on the SkillzBridge logo colours

All three use the same brand colours from the logo: orange, royal blue, charcoal and water blue. They differ in which colour leads, typography, shape and depth. Every text pair passes WCAG AA in light and dark. Logo orange is too light for white text, so orange buttons carry dark text, and orange text uses a deeper shade (#A8460A). "Switch to dark" previews dark mode. The wordmark is a text placeholder until I have the logo file.

Direction A

Bridge: blue-led, calm and trustworthy

Logo blue leads every action, so the product feels dependable to graduates, parents and HR managers alike. Orange is the spark: it marks the current step, highlights and celebrations. The headline font is Lexend, a rounded geometric face that echoes the logo's friendly wordmark, and it was designed for reading ease.

Direction B

Workshop: orange-led, energetic and hands-on

Leads with the logo's orange, the colour of the gear and the trades. Blue carries links and secondary actions. Bold grotesque headings, crisp charcoal offset shadows and arch shapes make it feel like a confident African creative workshop. Speaks most directly to young graduates and to the 'Trades' side of the brand.

Direction C

Blueprint: charcoal-led, precise and calm

The bridge's charcoal frames the interface, logo blue drives actions and orange is reserved for status and the current step. Tight grotesque headings, monospaced data labels, hairline borders instead of shadows and a faint blueprint grid, in the spirit of Linear, Stripe and Mercury. Best for dense company and admin dashboards.
